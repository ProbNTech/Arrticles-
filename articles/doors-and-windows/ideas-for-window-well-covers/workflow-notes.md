## Competitor gap insight
The top results for "ideas for window well covers" include vocal.media, Rockwell, Fab Glass & Mirror, DIY Sprout, WellExpert, WooHome, Zavza Seal and Window Well Experts. Most are listicles that mix two things: decorating the well (planters, fairy and rock gardens, faux scene liners, string lights) and a few cover materials (polycarbonate, an angled 2x4 frame). They also mix decor ideas with product promotion.

The gap is that few of them tie each idea to egress safety. That includes the IRC rule that a cover must release from inside without keys, tools or special knowledge, plus the push-open weight problem for DIY covers. They also rarely separate cover ideas from well-decor ideas, or give a quick way to choose. Our edge is a numbered list of 12 ideas grouped by goal (light, strength, DIY, decor), framed around safety first, with a decision table and real price benchmarks.
