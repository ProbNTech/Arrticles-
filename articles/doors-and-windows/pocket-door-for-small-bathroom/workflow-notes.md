## Competitor gap insight
Top results for the keyword were Tinyhouse.com ("Yay or Nay?"), Houzz photo galleries, Robern, Coohom, House Digest, Lost At E Minor (22 ideas), Johnson Hardware news, DIYCraftsy (24 ideas), United Porte and a Walmart product page. That is more than five relevant pages, so the sample is adequate.
- Most of these pages are **idea galleries or listicles** (frosted glass, mirrored, shiplap, barn-style). They focus on looks and claim "saves X sq ft". The square-footage figures disagree from site to site (9–12, 14 and 15 sq ft were all quoted).
- Few of them cover **what the wall must have** (an empty cavity, the header, load-bearing concerns), **what the reader gives up** (wall space for towel bars and outlets, sound and odor privacy), or **real 2026 costs split by new construction vs. retrofit**.
- **Content edge:** a decision guide rather than an inspiration gallery. It covers the trade-offs, a wall-requirements check with safety framing, sourced costs, and a practical first step (find out what's inside the wall).
