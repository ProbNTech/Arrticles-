## 4. FAQ schema suggestions
Questions below are not answered directly in the body. Answers use only facts already in the article or its cited sources.

1. **Can I install a pocket door myself?** Fitting a pocket door into a fully open wall is a common DIY job. Adding one to a finished wall means opening the wall and possibly moving plumbing or wiring, so it is safer to hire a licensed contractor, and an electrician for any wiring.
2. **Does a 32-inch pocket door cost less to retrofit than a 36-inch one?** Usually, yes. Fixr lists about $1,350 to $2,200 for a 32-inch retrofit, compared with $1,550 to $2,950 for a 36-inch door.
3. **What can I hang on the wall over a pocket door?** Only light items. The pocket section is hollow, so towel bars, cabinets, heavy mirrors, switches, and outlets have to go on a different section of wall.
4. **What happens if the wall turns out to have mold or asbestos?** Fixr notes that opening a wall can uncover mold or asbestos, and either one needs professional remediation before the pocket door work can continue.
