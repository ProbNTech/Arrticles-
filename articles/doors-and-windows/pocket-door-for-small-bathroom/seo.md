## 4. FAQ schema suggestions
Questions below are not answered directly in the body. Answers use only facts already in the article or its cited sources.

1. **Can I install a pocket door myself?** Adding a pocket door to a finished wall means opening the wall and possibly moving plumbing or wiring, so hire a licensed contractor, and a licensed electrician for any wiring. If the wall is load-bearing, a contractor or structural engineer should handle the header, and you should check local permit rules first.
2. **Does a 32-inch pocket door cost less to retrofit than a 36-inch one?** Usually, yes. Fixr lists about $1,350 to $2,200 for a 32-inch retrofit, compared with $1,550 to $2,950 for a 36-inch door.
3. **How heavy a door can a pocket door frame hold?** It depends on the frame kit, so check its weight rating against your door. As one example, Johnson Hardware's 1500SC Series soft-close frame kit is rated for doors up to 165 pounds.
4. **What happens if the wall turns out to have mold or asbestos?** Fixr notes that opening a wall can uncover mold or asbestos, and either one needs professional remediation before the pocket door work can continue.
