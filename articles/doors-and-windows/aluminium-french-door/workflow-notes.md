## Competitor gap insight
The top results for "aluminium French door" are mostly UK manufacturer and retailer product pages (Origin, Climadoor, Doors Online UK, External Doors Online, Vufold) plus a few US product pages (Eris Home Products, Arcadia Custom, Oridow, Home Center Outlet). They focus on styles (Georgian, Soho), colors, multi-point locks, and GBP prices. Pros/cons articles (e-architect, TGP Systems, Doors and Beyond) are generic and repeat unsourced claims (e.g., a "30% energy loss" figure attributed to DOE, a 30–50 year lifespan).

**Content edge:** a neutral, US-focused guide that (1) explains the thermal break as the deciding factor and how to read U-factor/SHGC, (2) gives US dollar costs from US cost guides, (3) is honest that plain aluminum suits moderate climates, and (4) covers swing direction, safety glazing, impact ratings, and structural/permit cautions. Few top results do this for US readers. The UK-heavy SERP is itself a signal: the British spelling is searched, but US-centric content is thin.
