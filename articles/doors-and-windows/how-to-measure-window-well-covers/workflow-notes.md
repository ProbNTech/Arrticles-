## Competitor gap insight
The ranking results included Window Well Supply, Guardian Covers, windowwell.com (calculator), Window Well Experts (blog + PDF guide), Well Expert, Feldhawk, coverUP Window Wells, and several thin pages.dev/AI-style pages. Almost all of them are **manufacturer pages** that cover the same basics: width along the house, projection from center, shape, a lip/overhang, and 1/8-inch precision. More than 5 relevant pages exist, so this is not a thin SERP. The gaps:
- Few explain **why** you measure the outer rim, not the opening, or the "raw well size vs. finished cover size" overhang confusion.
- Out-of-square and warped wells (multiple widths plus diagonals) are covered thinly.
- **Egress** is usually a separate sales page. Little ties IRC well minimums, ladders and swing clearance back to *measuring*.
- No neutral, non-seller voice. No real homeowner account of measuring an irregular well (the cardboard-template approach).
Content edge: a brand-neutral, step-by-step guide with a mistakes and egress layer and a real DIY template account.
