## Competitor gap insight
Ranking pages from the searches: This Old House, Lowe's, Home Depot, Instructables, Pergo, Baird Brothers, Maner Builder Supply, Barnum Floors, nailerguy.com, reallycheapfloors.com, and several flooring-store DIY guides. That is more than 5, so this is a normal-competition SERP. They all cover the same sequence: acclimate, prep, chalk line, face-nail starter rows, blind-nail the field, stagger, face-nail the last rows, trim.

What most miss or cover poorly:
1. **Measured readiness checks.** They say "acclimate X days" but rarely give the NWFA moisture-difference test (4% for strip under 3", 2% for planks 3" and wider) or numeric flatness tolerances. Our edge is "test, don't guess," with the NWFA numbers.
2. **Choosing the method from the subfloor.** Retail guides push one method. Few explain clearly why the subfloor decides between nail, glue, and float.
3. **Structural red flags.** Almost none say to stop and call a pro when joists sag or are damaged.
4. **Real DIYer lessons.** Store guides have no third-party accounts.
