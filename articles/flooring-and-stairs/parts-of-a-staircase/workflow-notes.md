Gap / our edge (based on search summaries, not full reads):
1. Few tie the names to **why they matter to a US homeowner**: describing a problem to a contractor, comparing quotes, ordering parts.
2. UK-heavy results; few pair the terms with **US IRC numbers** (riser, tread, nosing, handrail height) and the "codes vary locally" caveat.
3. **Banister / baluster / spindle / balustrade confusion** is spread across separate articles rather than settled in the main guide.
4. Glossaries rarely group parts by job (steps, structure, railing, layout, decorative), which makes them hard to scan.
5. None surfaced a real homeowner or builder perspective.
