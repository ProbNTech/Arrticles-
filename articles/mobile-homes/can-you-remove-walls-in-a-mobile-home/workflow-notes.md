More than 5 relevant results exist.

**What they cover:** All of them say "yes, unless the wall is load-bearing." They name the exterior walls and the marriage line as structural and say to "consult an engineer."

**What they miss or cover poorly:**
1. Few explain shear walls in single-wides, which are wind bracing rather than roof support.
2. None cite the actual federal rule, HUD 24 CFR Part 3285: contact the LAHJ before alterations, and no added loads unless the change is engineer-designed.
3. Several, such as "six-step guide", lean toward DIY demolition steps even for structural walls.
4. Few give a practical way to identify walls: the data plate and serial number, the original floor plan, and ceiling-height changes.
5. Costs are absent or generic.

**Our edge:** A safety-first, regulation-aware explainer that sorts walls by type and points readers to the right professional, without structural how-to steps.
