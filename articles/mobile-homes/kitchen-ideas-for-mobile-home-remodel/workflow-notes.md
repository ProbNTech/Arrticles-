## Competitor gap insight
The brief is confirmed: informational intent, 1,400–1,500 words, mobile/manufactured home owners, warm 7th–9th grade tone, US localization, and the safety rule.

The top results were e-architect, Mobile Home Parts Store, Mobile Home Living, Coohom, Your White Knight, Creative Design, BuildGreenNH, Fairfax Kitchen & Bath, and Jack Cooper. More than 5 relevant articles rank, so this is not a thin field. Most are listicles that repeat the same ideas: paint cabinets, swap hardware, open shelving, light colors, pendant and under-cabinet lighting, peel-and-stick backsplash, an island made from old cabinets or a dresser, and a rough $5k–$15k budget.

**Gap:** Few of them explain *why* mobile home kitchens need different choices. Those reasons are thin 2x3/1x2 studs, cabinet and countertop weight, plumbing routed through the floor and belly, and the HUD code versus local permits. Even fewer tie each idea back to those limits or flag electrical, gas, and structural safety. Real, attributable owner projects are also rare. Our edge is ideas filtered through how the home is built, with safety notes and two real documented makeovers.
