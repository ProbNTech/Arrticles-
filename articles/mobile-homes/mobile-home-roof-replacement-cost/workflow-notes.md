## Competitor gap insight
Top-ranking pages: Fixr, Angi, HomeGuide, MHVillage blog, Homeyou, Peak Mobile Home Roofing, Southern National Roofing, Connell Roofing, HomeAdvisor. That is more than 5 relevant results, so this is a normal competitive SERP. All of them lead with a national average and a by-material or by-width table.

What they miss or cover poorly:
1. **Their numbers disagree** (Fixr averages $5,500, Angi about $5,000, HomeGuide's double-wide ranges go up to $16,000), and none explains why. Our edge: a side-by-side source table plus an explanation of the assumptions behind each figure.
2. **Structural load and the HUD data plate.** The cost guides barely mention that manufactured home roofs are engineered for a set roof load zone. That matters before choosing metal or a roof-over.
3. **Recoat vs. repair vs. replace** as a decision, not just a coating price line.
4. **Real owner accounts.** No cost guide includes them.
