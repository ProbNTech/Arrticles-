## Competitor gap insight
Top-ranking results (CRD Design Build "Complete Guide to House Lifting Methods", Rogue Engineer, Cracked Slab "Deep Dive", Coohom, Wikipedia "House raising", Cedreo, Model Remodel, ResearchGate study) mostly cover hydraulic jacking, cribbing and slab jacking, plus cost ranges. More than 5 relevant articles exist, so this is a competitive term.
Gaps: (1) few clearly separate full elevation from foundation leveling/slabjacking, which confuses readers; (2) slab-on-grade homes (lift with slab vs. detach) are covered thinly despite FEMA guidance on the feasibility limits; (3) almost none help the reader match a method to their own house type; (4) real, attributable homeowner outcomes and the timeline/temporary-housing burden are missing; (5) several competitor pages read like DIY guides ("Jacking Up Your Home: A DIYer's Guide"), so a clear safety-first, defer-to-pros framing is a differentiator.
Content edge: a method-by-house-type decision aid built on FEMA guidance, with real homeowner accounts and honest cost/time ranges.
