## 4. FAQ schema suggestions
These cover follow-up questions the body does not answer in a dedicated section. Answers use only information established in the article.

1. **Can I lift my house myself to save money?** No. House lifting is structural work that can cause serious structural damage or injury if done wrong, so it should be done by a licensed, insured house-lifting contractor working from a structural engineer's plan.
2. **Is house lifting the same as foundation leveling?** No. Leveling (with piers or slab jacking) corrects settlement in place, while house lifting raises the whole structure so a new or taller foundation can be built underneath.
3. **Does raising my house lower flood insurance?** It can. Elevating a home can significantly reduce flood insurance premiums, depending on the elevation level and flood zone.
4. **Who disconnects the utilities before a house is lifted?** Gas, electric, water and sewer lines should be disconnected and reconnected by licensed plumbers, electricians and the utility companies, never by the homeowner.
