## Competitor gap insight
More than 10 relevant pages rank (Angi, HomeGuide, Suncoast Enclosures, Mitchell Construction, Lakeside Renovation, Envy Home Services, Choice Home Remodeling, Exterior Additions, Modern Glazing, MyDeck, Block Renovation, Dave Fox, and others). Most are written by contractors and cover the same ground: seasonal use, single- vs. dual-pane glass, insulation, HVAC, and a cost range.

Gaps and weak spots:
1. **Codes.** Few explain that the IRC uses Sunroom Categories I–V rather than "3-season/4-season," or that the IECC has separate rules for "thermally isolated" sunrooms.
2. **Appraisal and square footage.** Few explain ANSI Z765 gross living area, which is why a 3-season room usually doesn't add square footage.
3. **Real owner experience.** Contractor pages rarely include homeowner accounts, such as the common regret of trying to heat a 3-season room after it's built.
4. **Retrofit reality.** Conversion is covered on separate pages, not inside the comparison.

Our edge: a neutral, non-sales comparison that covers code categories, appraisal treatment, and real forum accounts.
