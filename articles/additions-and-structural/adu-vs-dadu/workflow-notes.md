## Competitor gap insight
Brief confirmed: informational intent (functionally a comparison), 1,500–1,600 words, US homeowner audience, AdSense, safety rule applies (structural/electrical/plumbing work in conversions and new builds).

Top-ranking results for "adu vs dadu": RenoFi, The Madrona Group, United Signature, TrueBuilt Home, RG Pro Builders, Presite, Love Construction Seattle, Hines Group Homes, HomeNation, plus CRD Design Build, Intrust Funding, Board & Vellum, Bicknell Builds and Tacoma DADU. That's more than 5, so this is not a thin SERP. Almost all are builder or lender marketing pages, and most are Seattle-centric. They cover: definitions (DADU is a subset of ADU), privacy, design flexibility, rough cost ranges with no named source, and "DADUs earn more rent," also without data.

**Gaps / our edge:**
1. Cost figures are almost never sourced or dated. We use a named research report (UC Berkeley, 2021) next to a current builder price sheet and explain why they differ (region and time).
2. Few explain the naming confusion. "ADU" is the umbrella term, but in everyday talk it often means attached, and Seattle splits it into AADU and DADU.
3. Rules are presented as if they apply everywhere. We frame Seattle, Washington State and Portland as clearly labeled snapshots and send readers to their local planning department.
4. No real homeowner accounts. We use two attributed Seattle Times family stories.
5. No practical decision checklist, and no safety framing around structural work.
