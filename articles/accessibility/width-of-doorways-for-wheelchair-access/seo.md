## 4. FAQ schema suggestions

1. **Can I widen a doorway myself?** Swapping in offset hinges or removing a door and its stops is a common DIY step. Widening the opening means cutting studs and often adding a new header, so hire a licensed contractor and check with your local building department first.
2. **Do I need a 36-inch door to get 36 inches of clear width?** No. A hinged door's thickness and the doorstop take up part of the opening, so the clear width is always less than the door size; measure from the face of the open door to the opposite stop.
3. **Is a pocket or barn door better for wheelchair access?** It can help where a swinging door blocks the approach or the space beside the latch, because the door slides out of the way instead of swinging into the room.
4. **Do offset hinges work on any door?** They use the existing frame and add up to about 2 inches of clear width, which is often enough when a chair is just barely too wide; they will not help when the gap is larger than that.
