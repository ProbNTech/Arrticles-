## Competitor gap insight
More than 5 relevant ranking pages exist (1800wheelchair, SRAlab, Inclinator, Cambridge Doors, Vortex Doors, MobilityDeck, World Seniors, Nurture Mobility, Angi). They consistently cover: ADA 32-inch minimum, 36 inches as "recommended," typical wheelchair widths, and offset hinges.

Gaps / weak spots:
- Few clearly explain that 32 inches is *clear width*, not door size, and almost none cite the Fair Housing guideline that a 34-inch door is what delivers a nominal 32-inch opening.
- Most skip the ADA rule that openings deeper than 24 inches need 36 inches.
- Most say "ADA requires" without telling homeowners the ADA generally does not apply to private single-family homes.
- Latch-side maneuvering clearance and turning space are rarely tied back to the doorway decision.
- Many are written by product sellers; few give an honest cheapest-to-biggest fix ladder plus safety guidance for structural widening.
Content edge: "measure clear width, not the door" + full route (latch side, hallway, turning space) + fix ladder with safety and cost.
