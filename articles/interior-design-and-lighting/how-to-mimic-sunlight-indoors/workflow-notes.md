## Competitor gap insight
More than 5 relevant ranking pages exist. Apartment Therapy, Home & Texture, Optimize Your Biology, BlissLights, The Kitchn, Tom's Guide, LightSupplier, Lighting R Us, Prosky Panels and others all rank, so the gap analysis is valid. Ranking pages mostly give listicles of tricks: mirrors, light or eggshell paint, gloss finishes, sheer instead of heavy curtains, daylight/5000K bulbs, LED ceiling panels as fake skylights, and layered task plus general lighting. One health-oriented page (Optimize Your Biology) pushes 1,000+ lux, plus UV and infrared bulbs.

**Gaps and our content edge:**
1. Few pages explain the three specs that make light look like sun (Kelvin, CRI and brightness in lumens or lux) in plain words.
2. Pages rarely cover time-of-day shifting: bright and cool by day, warm and dim at night, grounded in circadian research.
3. None pair the tips with safety guidance for hardwired panels or roof work such as solar tubes.
4. Pages don't separate renter-friendly options from homeowner options.
5. Pages don't caution against home UV sources.
