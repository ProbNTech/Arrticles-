## Competitor gap insight

**Ranking pages reviewed (via search results):** HomeAdvisor ("18 Eye-Catching Updates"), Havenly (14 tips without a remodel), The DIY Playbook (12 easy ways), McCarter Design, Medford Design-Build, BLB Design Build, HGTV ("40 Things That Make Your House Look Dated"), Homemade by Carmona (budget), GreatBuildz (15 ways), Extra Space blog, Reborn Renovations, Block Renovation, plus several small-blog listicles. That is well over 5, so the competition is normal.

**What they cover:** paint (walls, trim, cabinets, doors, brick), lighting and statement fixtures, cabinet and door hardware, switch plates, window treatments and curtain rods, flooring (LVP, laminate, wood), decluttering and minimalism, mirrors, bathroom fixture swaps, cabinet refacing, open floor plans, area rugs.

**Gap / our edge:**
1. **No order of operations.** Almost all are flat listicles. None tells the reader what to do first by effort, cost, and impact. Our answer: a priority table plus "free and $ first, one room at a time."
2. **No way to diagnose.** Pages list fixes but don't help readers see what dates *their* rooms. Our answer: the doorway-photo exercise and a short list of what makes a home look dated.
3. **No cohesion rule.** Few explain that a modern look comes from repeating 1–2 metal finishes, a short palette, and a consistent wood tone from room to room.
4. **Weak safety and sourcing.** Lighting swaps and "open floor plan" tips usually come with no electrician, permit, or load-bearing-wall warning, and few hard numbers or cited data. Our answer: safety notes plus NAR 2025 and DOE citations.
5. **No real homeowner voices.** None documented a real, attributable account (our section is still a placeholder, see below).
