## Competitor gap insight
Competition is heavy: more than 10 relevant pages rank, including closet companies (California Closets, Closet America, Inspired Closets, Closet World, TCClosets, Vallier, Artisan) and home-services guides (Angi, HomeGuide, Homenish, Homedit, Livingetc). Most of them repeat the same size list (4x4 minimum, 5x5 recommended, 7x10 common, 10x10 for an island) and layout-by-layout sizes.

Where they fall short:
- They disagree on the minimum (4x4 vs 5x5) and on aisle width (24 vs 36 inches), and rarely explain why. Our edge is a simple formula: storage depth per wall + walkway = minimum width, so readers can check any space themselves.
- They rarely mention drawer pull-out clearance, accessibility (only Inspired Closets mentions the ADA 30 x 48 inch clear space), or the safety side of carving a closet out of a bedroom (load-bearing walls, wiring for lights). The brief's category is lighting, so closet lighting needs a safety-first mention.
- Many are sales pages that end in "book a consultation." A neutral, no-CTA guide is the gap for an AdSense site.

## Full audit report (all 13 items)
1. **Repeated ideas.** The first draft repeated the Inspired Closets "step-in" and "30 to 36 inch" points in three sections, and the conclusion restated the formula. Fixed: the step-in and cramped points now appear only in the designer section, the walkway H3 uses HomeGuide, and the conclusion now focuses on protecting the aisle.
2. **Fluff.** Cut "So the minimum is a starting line… The next step is…", "Each of these needs care.", and "on graph paper before you call anyone" (shortened).
3. **Generic AI phrases.** None of the banned phrasing remains. "Here's the simple formula" was kept because it introduces concrete content.
4. **Human voice.** The layout table area read like a catalog, so the H3s use "you" and practical uses. Acceptable.
5. **Readability.** Sentences are short. The long double-sided math sentence was split. Estimated grade level is 7–8.
6. **Keyword.** "walk-in closet dimension" appears in the first 100 words, and variants (walk-in closet dimensions/size, closet layout) are spread out. Not stuffed.
7. **Entity coverage.** All CORE entities are covered. SUPPORTING terms are used naturally: reach-in closet, existing closet, drawer, open shelves, storage needs, master bedroom, closet company, closet storage, wrap-around, island in the middle, sides of the closet, 10 feet, wider than a single-sided closet. Not used: the "40 inches and 80 inches" phrasing (covered as 80/40 rod heights), "suspended from the floor" (did not fit), and "custom closet" (only in the internal links). No missing CORE gap.
8. **Intent match.** Informational guide with a scannable table. Matches.
9. **Third-party experience.** The designer insight is attributed to Inspired Closets, a real source with a link. A homeowner account could not be found because searches ran out, so [USER EXPERIENCE NEEDED] stays.
10. **Expertise signals.** Specific inch measurements, a sizing formula, and clearance trade-offs (24 vs 36 inches around an island). Good.
11. **Safety.** The wall and lighting paragraphs both carry licensed-professional lines and [VERIFY] on code claims. No code minimum is stated as fact.
12. **Fact-check list / tags:** minimum 4x4 and 5x5 (HomeGuide); 24 + 24 (Inspired Closets); 4x5 one person (Angi); 24 in hanging and 20 in tight (Angi, HomeGuide); 24 in min and 30–36 comfortable walkway (HomeGuide); drawer clearance [VERIFY]; 80/40 rod heights [PARTIAL SOURCE]; long-hang height [VERIFY]; layout table (HomeGuide, California Closets, Angi); L-shaped corner tip [EXAMPLE NEEDED]; island 24 in (HomeGuide) vs 36 in (California Closets); two-person 6x10/7x10/100 sq ft (Inspired Closets); 7x7 double-sided (Angi); ADA 30x48 (Inspired Closets); lower or pull-down rods [VERIFY]; load-bearing/permit [VERIFY]; NEC lighting [VERIFY]; cost [COST NEEDED]; homeowner account [USER EXPERIENCE NEEDED].
